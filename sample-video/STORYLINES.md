# Storylines (sample video for Yapifier: the "No Hands" challenge)

Run with no stops: the recommendation was built; the other two are kept for a later switch.

## A. Keys go quiet (recommended: a stranger follows "typing → talking" without explanation)
- Device + form: life before and after, as an object-to-object chain on one yellow desk.
- What you see: a row of chunky keycaps types a prompt key by key; the text piles into a wall; every key drops away except Fn, which grows, presses down, and a purple voice wave rises out of it and becomes the title. Facts, the prize and the call to action are built from the same keycaps.
- First 2 s: a bare yellow ground with one small text cursor blinking twice (deliberately slow: Yapifier's "cut this pause" target).
- Beats: dead air → typing (hook) → wall of text (tension) → Fn held, wave, "No Hands" (reveal) → own work, by voice, 16→30 Oct (proof) → judged on 4 things, small print (proof) → ₹1,00,000 × 3 (payoff) → register + contact (claim/CTA) → end card with the "sample" disclaimer.
- Look: flat warm yellow ground, off-white keycaps with a dark legend, one purple accent for Fn and the voice.
- Sound: soft synthesized pulse under a calm narrator; key ticks on presses; one riser into the prize.

## B. One sentence becomes the challenge
- Type carries the argument, cut on the beat: a single spoken sentence assembles word by word in giant type, each word swapping the colour field.

## C. A day without typing
- A moment in someone's day: time stamps (9:00 stand-up, 13:00 bug report, 18:00 release notes) each answered by "say it" instead of typing.
